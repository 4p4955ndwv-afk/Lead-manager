Lead Manager Blueprint

A private lead and client system for organisations that win clients through Instagram and TikTok DMs. Claude answers every DM in seconds using what worked in your past conversations. When a phone number appears, a named person gets the lead straight away. The system then follows the client through appointments, treatment plans with several procedures, sessions, aftercare and return visits.

Channels

Official APIs only

Instagram Messaging API through your own approved Meta app. TikTok Business Messaging API where your region allows it. No scraping, no shared passwords.

Past DMs

Official data exports

The APIs return only the last 20 messages of a chat, so the full history comes from each platform's “Download your data” JSON file.

Brain

Claude in three places

Claude writes replies in real time, learns from history in batch jobs, and watches and runs the whole system through your own MCP server.

Platform

Cloudflare and one app

Workers, D1, R2 and Queues on Cloudflare's free tier to start. One web app that installs on phones, later wrapped as private iOS and Android apps.

People

Humans own the call

A shared number triggers an alert, an AI call brief and a 15‑minute clock for a named coordinator. Any stage can be overridden, with the reason logged.

§02 · Client journey

From first DM to returning client

  1. AI

    DM received

    Instant reply in seconds. Records which post, video, story or ad started the chat, and the language used.

    Exitsspam · existing client (opens their record)

  2. AI

    AI qualifies

    Finds out what they want, when and where. Answers FAQs and price ranges from the approved list. Checks age before anything is booked.

    Exitsnurture (not ready) · not a fit · under 18

  3. System

    Contact captured

    Number shared, or they asked for yours. The lead is created, merged with any existing record and assigned by rota. Claude writes a call brief.

    Exitsduplicate merged

  4. Coordinator

    Call & confirm

    Call within the SLA, log the outcome with one tap, agree the date and when payment or deposit is due.

    Exitsno answer ×3 → WhatsApp, then nurture · lost (reason)

  5. Front desk

    Appointment booked

    Deposit link, confirmation, reminders, directions and preparation notes go out automatically.

    Exitsreschedule loop · cancelled · refund

  6. Clinician

    Consultation

    Attended or no-show is recorded. Assessment notes and suitability stay in restricted fields.

    Exitsno-show → rebook · not suitable · thinking it over

  7. Clinician

    Treatment plan

    One plan holds several procedures, each with its own session count, price, package and payment plan, plus consent forms.

    Exitsdeclined (reason) · accepts part of the plan

  8. Team

    Sessions in progress

    Every session is scheduled and tracked (“Session 2 of 6”), with check-ins between sessions. Procedures can be added mid-way.

    Exitspaused · clinical escalation · partial refund

  9. System

    Complete & aftercare

    Final review, aftercare schedule, results photos in restricted storage, review and referral request.

    Exitscomplaint → manager

  10. AI + team

    Alumni & returns

    Check-ins at agreed intervals where the person consented. A returning DM is recognised instantly and opens a new episode on the same record.

    Exitsdo-not-contact · erase on request

Overrides are built in.

Anyone with permission can move a client to any stage, reopen a lost lead, edit a plan, change an SLA for one case or pause the AI for one person. The system asks for a reason and logs who did what and when.

§03 · Learning from past DMs

Turning years of converted chats into a playbook

Why not pull history through the API?

Meta's API returns only the 20 most recent messages of each conversation, and skips chats in the Requests folder that have been inactive for 30 days. Unofficial scrapers that read more break the platforms' terms and regularly get accounts banned. The data-export files contain the full history and are the platforms' own approved route.

  1. Export

    Instagram: Accounts Center → Download your information → Messages, all time, JSON. TikTok: Settings → Download your data → JSON. Download links expire after 4 days.

  2. Upload privately

    An admin uploads the ZIP files to a private R2 bucket in your Cloudflare account. Only the analysis job reads them.

  3. Clean and mask

    Conversations are parsed and names, numbers, emails and handles are replaced with tokens before Claude sees them. The key stays in your database.

  4. Mark outcomes

    Each chat is matched to your booking or payment records: became a client, shared a number but didn't book, or went cold. This is what lets us learn what converts.

  5. Analyse every chat

    Claude reads each conversation through the Batch API (half price) and records reply speed, opener, questions asked, objections, how price came up, when the number was requested and where people dropped off.

  6. Compare winners and losers

    Claude compares converted and lost chats across thousands of examples and writes Playbook v1: openers, qualifying questions, objection answers, when to ask for the number, and phrases to avoid.

  7. Approve

    Your team edits and approves the playbook in the app. A clinician signs off anything clinical. Only then does it go live.

  8. Keep learning

    Every week Claude reviews new chats and outcomes and proposes changes with evidence. Wording can be A/B tested. Nothing changes without approval.

§04 · AI reply rules

What the AI does, and where it stops

The AI will

in scope
  • Reply within seconds, any time of day, in the person's language.
  • Say early on that they're chatting with an AI assistant and can ask for a person at any time.
  • Answer logistics: location, opening hours, process, prices and ranges from the approved list.
  • Ask the qualifying questions from the playbook and steer toward a phone call.
  • Spot a phone number or a request for yours, create the lead and alert the team.
  • Hand over to a human when unsure, upset, clinical or asked to; the chat is then paused for AI.

The AI will not

blocked
  • Give medical advice, judge suitability or discuss risks for a specific person. Those go to a qualified person.
  • Promise results, invent prices or offers, or name prescription-only medicines where the law forbids it.
  • Book anyone under 18 or continue with someone who says they are a minor.
  • Message anyone outside the platform's reply window or send cold outreach.
  • Pretend to be a named human member of staff.
  • Ask for card details in DMs. Payment links come from the payment provider after the call.
Instagram window

Free-form replies within 24 hours of the person's last message. A human (not the AI) can send support replies for up to 7 days with Meta's Human Agent tag.

TikTok window

Replies within 48 hours of the person's last message, and at most 10 messages per message they send.

Window closed

The AI stays silent. If the person had shared a number, a call task goes to a coordinator instead.

Rolling out autonomy safely

Stage 1 · Shadow

Claude drafts a reply for every DM, but staff reply as usual. We compare the drafts with what staff actually sent.

Move on when reviewers rate most drafts as good as or better than the human reply.

Stage 2 · Co-pilot

Claude's draft appears in the app; staff tap Send, or edit first. Every edit teaches the playbook.

Move on after two weeks with most drafts sent unedited.

Stage 3 · Autopilot

Claude sends on its own. Low-confidence, sensitive or clinical chats still go to a person. A kill switch pauses all AI replies at once.

Target state. Nightly Claude audits sample 50 chats.

§08 · Claude as the brain

Three ways Claude connects

Real time

Reply engine

The Claude API, with tools to look up the lead, prices, availability and the playbook, and to flag a handoff. Prompt caching keeps the playbook cheap to reuse on every message.

On demand

Your own MCP server

Connect it in Claude as a custom connector and ask: “Which leads shared a number today and haven't been called?”, “Move her session 3 to Thursday and tell her”, “Why did TikTok conversion drop this week?” Claude acts with the asking person's permissions, and asks before messaging any client.

On schedule

Claude jobs

Daily briefing, a nightly quality check of AI chats, weekly playbook proposals, a monthly performance report, and an alert if response times or conversion drift.

Existing Claude connectors that slot in alongside

  • Cloudflare Developer Platform (official connector): Claude can watch the Workers, database and logs that run the system.
  • Gmail (already connected on your account), Google Calendar and Calendly: email follow-ups and diary sync if the organisation already uses them.
  • Stripe and Twilio: payment links, SMS and calling, if those providers suit your country.
  • respond.io: official TikTok and Instagram messaging partner with its own hosted MCP server for Claude. Held in reserve as the TikTok bridge if direct API access is refused (see §10).
§09 · Existing tools

Could existing Claude connectors be the system instead?

Partly. Several tools cover the inbox or the sales pipeline and connect to Claude, but none models a client who books several procedures, attends sessions over months and comes back years later. A stack of them would split one journey across three subscriptions, and Claude would only see it through each tool's limited connector.

Verdict

Build the core yourself on Cloudflare and talk to Instagram and TikTok directly through their official APIs. Use connectors where they add something: Cloudflare for monitoring, Gmail and calendars for the team, Stripe or a local provider for payments, and respond.io only as a TikTok bridge if needed. The fastest stopgap, if you need DMs answered before the build is ready, is respond.io + HubSpot connected to Claude, accepting that the treatment journey would live in spreadsheets until the core ships.

§13 · Before we start

Questions for you

  1. Which country is the organisation registered in, and where are most clients?TikTok's DM API isn't available to accounts registered in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, and the country decides which privacy and health-data laws apply.
  2. What kind of procedures does it offer?Medical, cosmetic, dental, hair or other. This sets what the AI may say, age limits, advertising rules and whether HIPAA-style rules apply.
  3. Do you have records of which past DM leads became paying clients?A booking sheet, clinic software or call log is enough. Without outcomes we can only learn what was said, not what converted.
  4. Roughly how many DMs arrive per day, and how many staff will log in, in which roles?Sizes costs, the alert rota and the permission templates.
  5. Which tools are in use today?Calendar, clinic or patient software, payment provider, WhatsApp Business. We integrate with what works and replace what doesn't.
  6. How should payment work?Deposit at booking, full payment, instalments; which provider is available in your country.
  7. Which languages do the DMs come in?Claude handles most languages; the playbook needs examples for each.
  8. Are both accounts business accounts, and who has admin access to the Meta Business portfolio and TikTok Business Center?Needed for Business Verification and the API applications in week 1.
  9. Are you comfortable starting in co-pilot mode for two to four weeks?Recommended. Staff approve AI drafts before the AI replies on its own.
